Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use the minimum needed to run the store, plus optional cookies — only with your consent — to understand and improve it.

Essential cookies (always on)

Required for the cart, checkout, security and your cookie preferences. Without these the store can't function.

Analytics cookies (consent)

Help us count visits, see which pages and products interest people, and fix what's broken. We only activate these after you accept analytics cookies.

Functional cookies (consent)

Remember choices like currency and wishlist contents to make repeat visits smoother.

Advertising cookies (consent)

Measure whether our ads lead to purchases so we don't spend on what doesn't work. Only activated with your consent.

Managing your preferences

The cookie banner appears on your first visit. You can accept all, reject non-essential, or choose per category — and change your mind any time via "Cookie preferences" in the footer.

Consent management

Your choices are stored on your device and honoured on every visit. Analytics and marketing tools are configured so that no non-essential cookies drop before consent (where those tools support it).
