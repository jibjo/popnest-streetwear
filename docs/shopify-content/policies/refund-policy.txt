ELIGIBILITY
Most items can be returned within [RETURN WINDOW] of delivery if they are unused, in original condition, and in original packaging with all hardware and documentation included.

RETURN WINDOW
The return window is [RETURN WINDOW] from the delivery date shown by carrier tracking.

PRODUCT CONDITION
Items must be returned as received: unused, undamaged, and complete. Fixtures that have been installed, wired or painted may not be returnable unless defective.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
- Gift cards
- Items marked final sale at checkout
- Installed or modified electrical fixtures (unless defective)
- Custom or special-order items, if offered

RETURN SHIPPING RESPONSIBILITY
Return shipping is [CUSTOMER / HEAVENLY] responsibility for change-of-mind returns. For defective, damaged or incorrect items, Heavenly covers return shipping.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS
If an item arrives damaged or develops a defect covered by the 2-year limited lighting warranty, contact us within 48 hours of delivery (for transit damage) with photos and your order number. We will replace the item or refund you — your choice where stock allows.

WRONG ITEMS
Received something you did not order? Keep the item, photograph the label and contents, and contact us — we will ship the correct item and sort out the rest.

REFUND PROCESSING
Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original payment method within [REFUND PROCESSING TIME]. Your bank may take additional days to post the funds. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is our error.

EXCHANGES
Where stock allows, we support exchanges (for example, a different finish of the same sconce). Contact us before shipping anything back and we will arrange the swap.

CANCELLATIONS
Orders can be cancelled for a full refund before they ship — contact us as early as possible. Once a tracking number is issued, the order must go through the returns process instead.

HOW TO START A RETURN
Email support@heavenly.co with your order number, the item, and the reason. We will reply within one business day with instructions and the return address: [RETURN ADDRESS].
