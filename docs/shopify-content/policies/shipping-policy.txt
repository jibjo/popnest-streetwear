PROCESSING TIME
Orders are prepared and quality-checked within [PROCESSING TIME] after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.

ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIMES
Delivery estimates begin once your order ships:
- Domestic: 3–7 business days
- Europe / UK: 5–10 business days
- Rest of world: 8–16 business days
Live estimates are shown at checkout once your address is entered. These are estimates, not guarantees — carrier delays can occur.

SHIPPING DESTINATIONS
We ship to most countries. If your country is not available at checkout, contact support@heavenly.co — we add destinations as reliable carrier coverage is confirmed.

SHIPPING COSTS
Costs are calculated by destination and weight, shown before payment, and confirmed at checkout. Free shipping applies to qualifying orders at or above [FREE-SHIPPING THRESHOLD] where offered. Rates are based on our actual carrier and fulfilment costs.

DUTIES, TAXES AND CUSTOMS
Import duties, taxes and customs fees for international orders are the recipient's responsibility unless stated otherwise at checkout. Customs policies vary by country; contact your local customs office for details. Heavenly has no control over customs delays or additional charges.

TRACKING
Every order ships with tracking. You will receive a tracking number by email when your order ships; you can also use the Track Order page at any time.

DELAYS
If a carrier delay affects your order, we will email you as soon as we know. You can always reach a human at support@heavenly.co.

INCORRECT ADDRESSES
Please double-check your address at checkout. Orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer may not be recoverable; where recovery is possible, re-shipping costs are the customer's responsibility.

LOST PACKAGES
If tracking shows delivered but your package has not arrived, first check with household members and neighbours, then contact us within [LOST-PACKAGE CLAIM WINDOW]. We will open a carrier investigation and replace or refund confirmed-lost orders.

DAMAGED PACKAGES
If your order arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and product and contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will replace damaged items or refund your order — see our Refund Policy.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING LIMITATIONS
Some products may have destination restrictions due to carrier regulations. If a product cannot ship to you, it will be indicated at checkout before payment.
