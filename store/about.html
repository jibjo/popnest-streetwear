About Heavenly

Design is how it works in your home

Heavenly is an independent home décor brand built around one belief: the fastest way to change how a room feels is light.

Most lighting is sold one of two ways: beautiful but flimsy, or rugged but forgettable. We started Heavenly because we kept buying both — and returning both. A sconce that looked stunning in photos but buzzed within a month. A "bargain" pendant that arrived feeling like a toy.

So we took a different approach: a deliberately small collection, specified like engineering and finished like furniture. Every product page states the real materials, the exact dimensions, the wattage, the IP rating, the warranty. If a specification isn't confirmed yet, we say so instead of guessing. If a product isn't right for a room, we'd rather tell you than make a sale.

What we believe

  • Light is architecture. The right sconce doesn't just illuminate a wall — it gives it depth, warmth and presence.
  • Honest specifications. No mystery alloys, no invented discounts, no fake countdowns. What you read is what ships.
  • Small collection, high standard. We'd rather perfect eleven pieces than list eleven hundred.
  • Accessible premium. Direct-to-you pricing without the showroom markup — quality you can verify, at prices that make sense.

How we work

We design and curate in-house, work directly with manufacturers who meet our finish and electrical standards, and ship from fulfilment partners close to our customers. Between you and us there are no middlemen adding markup — just people who care whether your entry wall glows warmly when you come home.

Our standards are public on purpose: IP65 sealing for anything that meets weather, warm 3000 K LEDs as the default colour temperature, integrated long-life LED modules on outdoor fixtures, and a 2-year limited warranty on lighting. When we get something wrong, our support team makes it right.

"We don't sell light fixtures. We sell the feeling of coming home to a house that glows."

What's next

The collection grows slowly and deliberately: more outdoor pieces, dining and bedroom lighting, and the mirrors, rugs and objects that finish a room. Join the newsletter to see each release first — and if there's something you'd love us to make, tell us. We read everything.
