Every product lists exact height, width and depth, plus the projection from the wall. For sconces, a good rule: mount roughly eye height (about 66–72 inches / 168–183 cm to the centre), or beside a door with 12–18 inches of clearance from the frame.

Our sconces hardwire to a standard junction box — a confident DIYer can install them with the circuit off, following the included diagram. If in doubt, use a licensed electrician. Lamps and mirrors are tool-free or near-tool-free.
