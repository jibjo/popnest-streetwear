Accessibility

A beautiful store is one everyone can use.

Our commitment

We want every visitor to browse, buy and get help comfortably. This site is built with accessibility as a requirement, not an afterthought — and we treat it as an ongoing practice.

What we've built in

  • Semantic HTML with a proper heading hierarchy and landmarks
  • Full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and a "skip to content" link
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful imagery
  • Colour contrast checked against WCAG AA guidance
  • Accessible forms with labelled fields and clear error messages
  • Menus, drawers and dialogs that close with the Escape key and announce their role
  • Respect for the operating-system "reduce motion" setting — animations are disabled automatically
  • Text that reflows cleanly on small screens and zoom

Where we're improving

Accessibility is never "done". We review new pages and features before release, test with keyboard-only navigation, and work toward conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

Found a barrier?

Tell us — genuinely. Email support@heavenly.co with the page and what didn't work, and we'll respond within one business day and fix confirmed issues as a priority.
