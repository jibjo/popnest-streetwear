Installation

Both Arden finishes hardwire to a standard outdoor junction box. Turn off the circuit at the breaker, mount the bracket (vertical or horizontal — the head rotates), connect the leads per the included diagram, and seal. If you're not comfortable with basic electrical work, we recommend a licensed electrician.

Care

Wipe the aluminum body with a soft, damp cloth. Avoid abrasive pads, solvents and pressure washers. In coastal areas, a monthly rinse with fresh water keeps the finish at its best.

Compatibility

Suitable for covered and exposed outdoor walls — entries, patios, porches, garden and boundary walls. For oceanfront installations we recommend the Brushed Bronze finish.
